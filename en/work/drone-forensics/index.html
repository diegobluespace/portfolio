Drone Forensics Suite

Overview

A forensics suite used in investigation and security to detect illegal drone flights and secure digital evidence. It recovers data stored on the aircraft, controller, external media, and companion apps, then saves it and exports reports. It analyzes GPS flight logs, replays them in a viewer, helps determine the cause of incidents, and previews recovered media.

Problem

QA and customers kept reporting that “the features are far too slow.” One core feature took about three minutes to run, and users had no choice but to wait.

Approach

  • I pulled the slow core logic out of the UI process and moved it into a separate background process (an AOT-compiled console app).
  • I refactored and optimized that module and compiled it ahead of time (AOT). JIT cost at startup disappeared, and the binary became much harder to decompile.
  • I restructured the UI with MVVM so results appear as soon as they are ready.

What else improved

  • CI/CD: automated build, packaging, and release with TeamCity.
  • Collaboration: moved from SVN to GitLab and shortened code review.
  • Features: FFmpeg-based screen recording, and analysis that parses file formats into readable logs.

Results

  • Core feature runtime from about 3 min to about 60 ms, roughly 3,000× faster
  • A development plan scoped for about a year finished in six months

My scope

After taking over the product as its sole engineer in late 2022, I ran it end to end: planning meetings, Figma design, development, performance work, CI/CD, customer questions, and scheduling.
